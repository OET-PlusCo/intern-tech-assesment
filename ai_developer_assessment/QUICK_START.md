# Quick Start Guide for Interviewers

## 5-Minute Prep Checklist

### Before the Interview:
1. ✅ Review `README_HIRING_MANAGER.md` for full context
2. ✅ Print or open `INTERVIEW_SCORECARD.md` for scoring
3. ✅ Have `SOLUTION_GUIDE.md` open (for debugging answers)
4. ✅ Have `INTERVIEW_QUESTIONS.md` ready
5. ✅ Test screen sharing setup

---

## Interview Flow (60 minutes)

### Opening (2 min)
"Hi [Candidate], thanks for joining. Today's interview has two parts:
1. A 15-minute live debugging exercise where you'll fix a broken ML API
2. 45 minutes of technical questions about system design, MLOps, and problem-solving

Feel free to think aloud, ask questions, and admit if something is unclear. We value transparency and clear communication."

---

### Part 1: Debugging (15 min)

**Say**: "I'm sending you a file called `buggy_model_api.py`. This is a FastAPI service for a churn prediction model that's failing in production. Please share your screen and find and fix as many issues as you can in 15 minutes. Think aloud as you work."

**Share**: `buggy_model_api.py`

**Watch for**:
- Do they ask clarifying questions?
- Do they run/test the code mentally?
- Do they find multiple bugs or stop after one?
- Do they explain their reasoning?

**Refer to**: `SOLUTION_GUIDE.md` for bug details

---

### Part 2: Technical Questions (45 min)

#### System Design (15 min)
- Q1: Fraud detection system architecture
- Q2: Design decisions under constraints

#### MLOps (15 min)
- Q3: Model deployment strategy
- Q4: MLOps automation
- Q5: Monitoring & observability

#### Problem-Solving (15 min)
- Q6: Handling ambiguity
- Q7: Technical debt trade-offs
- Q9: Ownership & debugging

**Refer to**: `INTERVIEW_QUESTIONS.md` for full questions

---

### Closing (3 min)

"Do you have any questions for me?"

## Scoring

Use `INTERVIEW_SCORECARD.md` to score as you go.

### Quick Assessment:
- **90-100**: Strong Hire
- **75-89**: Hire
- **60-74**: Maybe
- **<60**: No Hire

---

## Red Flags to Watch For

🚩 **Stop signs**:
- Makes excuses or blames tools
- Doesn't ask clarifying questions
- Can't explain trade-offs
- Overconfident without substance
- Gives up easily

✅ **Green lights**:
- Asks great questions
- Admits uncertainty
- Systematic approach
- Thinks about production/end-users
- Takes ownership

---

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Don't give hints too early** - Let them struggle a bit to see problem-solving
2. **Ask "why"** - Don't accept surface answers
3. **Watch for depth** - Can they explain trade-offs and alternatives?
4. **Listen for soft skills** - Are they transparent, assertive, collaborative?
