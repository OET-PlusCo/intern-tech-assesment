## 🏆 Success Criteria

### Hire If:
- Finds 6+ bugs (especially ML-specific ones)
- Explains reasoning clearly
- Asks clarifying questions
- Designs scalable systems
- Knows MLOps practices
- Shows transparency & assertiveness
- **Score: 75+**

### Don't Hire If:
- Finds <3 bugs
- Can't explain impact
- Doesn't ask questions
- Shallow technical knowledge
- Makes excuses
- Overconfident without substance
- **Score: <60**

---

## 💡 Remember

1. **Create psychological safety** - Tell them it's okay to say "I don't know"
2. **Let them struggle** - Don't give hints too early
3. **Listen for soft skills** - Transparency, assertiveness, ownership
4. **Trust the process** - The assessment is designed to reveal true capabilities
